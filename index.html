Questions new business owners ask us

What does URise Hub do?

URise Hub is a digital marketing agency. We run Facebook Ads, grow Instagram accounts, optimize YouTube channels for search, manage social media pages and generate leads for businesses.

Can you help a brand-new business with no followers?

Yes. New businesses are who we build our launch process for. We set up your pages, plan your first offer and start reaching the right people from day one.

Which platforms do you work on?

We work on Facebook, Instagram and YouTube, and we build lead generation systems such as lead forms, landing pages and Messenger funnels around them.

How long does it take to see results?

Paid ads can bring your first messages and leads within days after they are approved. Organic growth and YouTube SEO take longer, usually a few months. We share the plan and timeline before we start.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the services you choose and your ad budget. Ad spend is paid to the platform and is separate from our service fee. Message us your goal and we will suggest a plan that fits.
